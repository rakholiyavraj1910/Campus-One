CampusOne is the unified digital campus portal for Government Polytechnic Ahmedabad (GPA) students and faculty. Access official circulars, academic notices, study materials, department directories, and student services.

Government Polytechnic Ahmedabad Services & Resources

  • Student Academic Portal: Track class attendance, view weekly lecture timetables, inspect internal continuous assessment marks, and download examination hall tickets.
  • Faculty Teaching Suite: Log daily classroom attendance, submit student internal evaluation marks, upload syllabus notes, and manage class rosters.
  • Official College Notices: Real-time circulars for GTU diploma examinations, hall tickets, administrative announcements, and fee payment schedules.
  • Study Materials Repository: Curated lecture notes, laboratory experiment manuals, syllabus documentation, and previous year question papers (PYQs).
  • Academic Departments: Computer Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Automobile Engineering, IT, and more.
